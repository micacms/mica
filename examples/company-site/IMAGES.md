# Generated example images

These images were made with the built-in OpenAI image model for this fictional
company website. People, workspaces and client scenes are fictional. They do
not show real staff, offices or client projects.

The files are compressed JPEG images, at most 1280 pixels wide, and the
portraits are 768 × 1024 pixels. This keeps the repository small. Astro makes the
responsive website images during the build. Each image has alt text in its
content entry. The studio, service desk and workspace images are stored in
`content/media/`. The other files are stored with their entries.

## Prompts

Each section records the full prompt used to make the image. Paths are relative
to `content/`.
